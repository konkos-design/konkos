    What owners usually ask first.

    Why is the concept free?

    Because showing is faster than explaining. I only build a concept when I already see what your online presence is missing, and most owners decide within a minute once they see their own business on a phone.

    What do I need to prepare?

    Nothing. I take photos, services, opening hours and reviews from your Google Maps listing and your Facebook or Instagram page. If you have better photos, send them later.

    What exactly do I get for 350 €?

    The full site, up to 5 pages, on your own domain: mobile-first, with your texts, photos and reviews, edits until you approve it, the domain and the launch. All files and source code are yours.

    Can the site be in my language, or in several?

    Yes. I build in English, Ukrainian or any language your customers speak, and I can make the site multilingual with a language switcher.

    Are there monthly fees?

    No. 350 € once. Hosting for a site like this can be free, and the domain is registered in your name, so you stay in control.

    How long does it take?

    The concept takes one day. The full site after your yes is usually ready within a week.

    Do we have to talk on the phone?

    No. Everything can be done in writing, in your language: WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook or email.

    What if I say no?

    Then you pay nothing. Every concept is marked as unofficial, and I take it down if you ask.

    You are in Ukraine. Is that a risk?

    Your site lives on hosting outside Ukraine, so nothing depends on my power grid. Payment is by invoice, and I answer within a day.
